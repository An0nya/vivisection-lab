§1Direction is read; magnitude is discarded

Pre-norm RMSNorm strips the overall scale off the residual stream before every block and before the output head. So the token the model emits depends on the direction of that stream, not its length. This one fact explains a whole cluster of otherwise-baffling results.

Scale a single layer's contribution — x + α·(block(x) − x) — at a low-contribution middle layer (L18) and nothing happens, across α from −1 to 2.5. The output is byte-identical. Do the same at a high-contribution layer near the output (L38) and any α, even 0.5, turns the output to soup. Magnitude knobs push on the one quantity the architecture is built to ignore — except where a layer's contribution is so large that scaling it rotates the direction too.

The per-layer fractional contribution ‖Δ‖/‖h‖ is U-shaped: high at the input layers (L0–2) and the output layers (L38–39), and a flat ≈0.22 plateau through the whole middle. That plateau is the residual multiplier, made visible. Scaling only bites where the curve is high.

Takeaway To change what a pre-norm model does, you have to change a direction, not a length. Every working intervention on this page — duplication, pruning, steering — moves direction. Every inert one only stretched a vector the next norm was about to discard.

§2Where noise is lethal — the depth U-curve

Inject Gaussian noise scaled to a layer's own output, one layer at a time, and ask where the model survives it. The vulnerability curve is the inverse of the contribution curve — and it draws the meaning map.

Robust at the ends. Noise at an early layer (L4) is denoised by the 36 layers downstream of it. Noise at a late layer (L36) lands on a representation that is already committed and high-norm — it barely moves. Both ends shrug it off.

Catastrophic in the middle. Noise injected at L10–L20 destroys the output. This is the load-bearing reasoning band, and every layer downstream amplifies the off-manifold perturbation rather than damping it. Damage is worst exactly where meaning lives.

8-bit buys an octave. The noise the model can absorb before breaking sits at σ≈1.0 at 8-bit and σ≈0.5 at 4-bit — roughly one octave of headroom. Quantization is itself injected noise, and 4-bit simply starts closer to its own error budget. The lesion map is identical at both precisions; precision only sets the texture of failure — 8-bit fails into tight repetition loops, 4-bit into "semantic teleport," confidently solving a different, neighbouring problem.

Noise off the manifold does far more damage than scaling along it. Scaling preserves a learned direction and is survivable; noise injects a random one that survives the norm and gets amplified by every layer that follows. Granite vivisection · noise injection

§3Duplication is function iteration, not scaling

A duplicated block recomputes block(RMSNorm(h+Δ)) on its own output. That moves the direction — it is a step of power iteration toward the block's dominant eigenvector. This is why duplication amplifies where scaling was inert, and why one layer copied eight or eleven times collapses to a single-token attractor. (Granite's brain-damage filler token, for the record, is important.)

Damping rescues the collapse. Treat the residual stream as a continuous-depth flow: each block is a velocity field, one application is an Euler step of size 1. Stacking k full copies is k steps — it overshoots. Decaying the later copies is ODE sub-stepping (steps 1.0, 0.5, 0.25…). The control variable is the total integration distance Σα, not the copy count: eight full copies (Σα=8) → soup; the same eight copies decayed to Σα≈2 → correct. Σα≈3.6 is the fraying edge.

And reasoning circuits have a length. Holding total depth fixed at 60 layers and varying only the size of the repeated sub-block, the score falls off a cliff between block-size 4 and 2 — re-running a block ≥4 layers long re-runs whole circuits (fine), while repeating in chunks of 1–2 fragments a circuit mid-computation (breaks). The coherence length of a reasoning circuit here is ≈4 contiguous layers.

Why it matters downstream "Dup = power iteration" is the mechanism behind the entire sweep below. It predicts that a single extra copy nudges the model toward a layer's dominant mode (sometimes useful) and that repeated copies run away to a fixed point (always fatal). The dose-response in §6 is that prediction, measured.

§4The layer map

The systematic pass: duplicate, then prune, each of the 40 layers, and score on a mixed battery of math, multiple-choice knowledge, and emotional-reasoning probes. But before any result — read the baseline, because it decides which domain can carry signal at all.

At 4-bit, no chain of thought, the battery is badly saturated — and that's not a bug to fix, it's the lens you read everything through:

N.B. A saturated baseline is the normal condition of a small base model (see the through-line). It's why the entire map below is really an EQ map.

The EQ boosters are not one band but several depths; L10 is the champion single layer (+8.98, MC untouched); L6 is a hard retrieval bottleneck (any touch obliterates MC); the input layers and the final layer crater both ways — the depth U-curve, behavioural edition.

EQ boosts at several depths

Duplicating L10 alone takes EQ from 0.181 to 0.749 while leaving MC at a perfect 1.000 — a clean, free gain. Boosters recur early-mid (L5, L9, L10), mid (L16, L17, L20), late (L24, L26, L29) and very-late (L33, L35, L38). Extra compute helps emotional reasoning at many points, not one band.

The rescue-prune layers

Some layers actively suppress EQ — removing them is a net win: L14 (prune eq +3.7), L15 (+2.9), L23, L32. Having exactly one copy of these is the worst of the three options. Same signature as the Gemma e4b interference layers.

L6, the bottleneck

prune_6 drives MC paired-t to −23.7 — it obliterates retrieval. Every block-prune containing L6 produces total model death. It is load-bearing in a way no other single layer is. The ends are the other fragile zone: dup_39 (MC −9.33) turns the output layer to soup.

§5Dose-response: medicine, then poison

"Dup = power iteration toward a fixed point" makes a sharp prediction: a little is good, more runs away to collapse. Stack 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 copies of a single EQ-winner layer and the prediction comes true on contact.

Fig 2 — the dose-response curve. EQ score against the number of copies of one winner layer. For every layer, ×2 (a single extra copy) is the peak; ×3 begins the decline; ×4 and beyond collapse EQ — and eventually MC — to the floor. The lethal dose sits just past the therapeutic one. L24 is the single exception in the whole series: its ×3 genuinely beats its ×2, a later low-delta layer that tolerates one more iteration before the runaway.

The cliff is sharp and layer-specific. L10 and L5 fall off it immediately — ×3 is already total EQ collapse. L17, L38 degrade more gently over ×3–×4 before the same fate. By ×5–×8, several configs drive even the ceilinged MC to exactly 0.000: total model death, the behavioural face of the single-token attractor from §3.

This is why every clean win in this sweep is either a single duplication or a combination of different layers — never repeated copies of one. The power-iteration runaway is local to a layer; spreading the extra compute across layers avoids it.

The clinical reading One extra copy is medicine. Two or more is poison. And the lethal dose is just past the therapeutic one — which is exactly why damping (sub-dividing that one Euler step into fractions) is the obvious next lever, and the first of the rounds that didn't get to run (§8).

Cross-depth stacking is superadditive

combo_10_38 pairs an early-mid layer with a very-late one and lands EQ +9.38 — higher than either single (dup_10 +8.98, dup_38 +7.09) — while keeping MC at a perfect 1.000, which dup_38 alone could not. The late layer's MC cost vanishes in combination. This is the granite analog of Gemma's cross-zone amplification: spread the surgery across depths and the gains compound while the costs cancel.

Generalist vs specialist is decided by which layer

Whether a combo is a generalist (EQ up, MC held) or an EQ-specialist (EQ up, MC paid) depends entirely on its members, not on the act of combining. L10, L17, L29 are clean. L33 above all spends retrieval for EQ — any combo containing it craters MC. Granite, like Gemma, has no single "make it smarter" axis: it has a generalist lever and a specialist lever, and the combo sweep is what tells them apart.

§7Steering & soft abliteration

Duplication and pruning move direction by rearranging the stack. Activation steering does it on purpose: take a difference-of-means direction over a contrast set, inject α·RMS·unit_dir at the mid band, and the output bends. Calibration is the whole game — injecting at six layers compounds roughly six-fold, so a usable α is 0.1–0.5 where a single layer needed ~1.5.

Soft abliteration works. A refusal direction from matched harmful/harmless pairs, steered at α≈0.25–0.5, flips refuse→comply cleanly with no degradation — a validated, runtime version of the weight-orthogonalization trick. The induce (+refusal) side is messier: the extracted axis is really "refusal + negativity," entangled with hostility, and soups faster.

The real lesson Direction quality is contrast-set quality. The injection is trivial; the experiment is the set. Loosely-matched "stance" pairs steered register (toxic-positivity, marketing copy), not stance. An "answer = 500" injection at α=0.15 didn't get accepted or rejected — it overwrote the premise ("a bookshop had 500 books"). You get out exactly the axis you actually isolated.
A volcano-minus-snowfall vector captured at L20 and added to a math prompt: a faint tint at low α, invented nonsense at α=1.5, and at α=8 the raw "Titan / Zeus" volcano semantics surface — at a layer where magnitude-scaling the same vector did nothing at all. §8Two crashes worth more than a result

The sweep died twice mid-run. The lesson from each outlived any single config.

Crash 1 — the OOM that was realconfig ~31/81
MLX's unbounded buffer-cache pool
The run died with no traceback. MLX's buffer cache balloons to ~3 GB during the heavy 256-token math batches and never returns it to the OS; on a 16 GB box already near 13.8 GB used, that's a SIGKILL. Fix: mx.set_cache_limit(1.5GB) at startup, mx.clear_cache() between configs. Footprint dropped from ~6 GB peak to ~4 GB. (Correct practice for any MLX sweep — it stayed in even though it turned out not to be what killed the run.)
Crash 2 — the one I misdiagnosed for an hourfault addr = 168
A ctypes out-buffer too small for the kernel
After the cache fix it crashed again — twice — with SIGSEGV deep in CPython core (_io_open, then json.dumps), nowhere near my code. The tell was the fault address: 168 in both. 168 is sizeof(rusage_info_v2). To report the honest memory footprint I'd called proc_pid_rusage(RUSAGE_INFO_V2) with an output struct declared at only 96 bytes. The kernel writes the full 168. Every batch overflowed the buffer by 72 bytes, quietly corrupting adjacent heap — and CPython then segfaulted in whatever allocated next.

The lesson: an undersized ctypes out-buffer for a syscall is silent heap corruption that surfaces as a crash in completely unrelated code. When a crash lands in core library code with a numeric fault address, check whether that number is a struct size.
Postscript — the damping round, complete

The second-order round finished all 469 configs, including the part the dose-response made urgent: damping — sub-dividing that one fatal extra Euler step into a fraction α. The answer is the most useful result of the round, and it's selective.

Damping buys back the MC cost. For the layers whose full copy carried a retrieval cost, a damped copy keeps the EQ gain and restores MC toward 1.0: damp_33_a0.5 lifts EQ to 0.49 — above the full dup_33's 0.46 — while pulling MC from 0.56 back to 0.94, and damp_5_a0.5 beats full dup_5 on both axes (EQ 0.59 vs 0.53, MC 1.00 vs 0.88). Damping is a precision tool that turns an EQ-specialist toward a generalist. For layers already clean (L10, L17) it only dilutes the gain — no benefit there.

It is collapse-proof. Where a second full copy kills the model (dose_10_x3 → EQ 0), a damped second copy stays alive (damp_10_a0.5 → EQ 0.65). The decaying-α "ODE" stacks confirm the vivisection's thesis: what matters is the total integration distance Σα, not the copy count — Σα≈0.75 survives, Σα≥1.5 collapses. And the L6 retrieval bottleneck, which any full touch obliterated, survives a gentle one: damp_6_a0.1 holds MC at a perfect 1.000.

Pruning never compounds cleanly. Removing the interference layers together doesn't add up — pairs containing L23 collapse EQ to zero, and every multi-prune dents MC. The "rescue" reshapes (dup the winners + prune the interference) all pay in retrieval; the all-in kitchen-sink collapses outright. The clean wins stay exactly what they were: pure cross-depth dup-combos like combo_10_38. Pruning is never free in combination. Still open: an 8-bit replication — the crater boundaries should move ~an octave, the booster zones should not.

§9Caveats

  • 5 seeds per config (15 for baseline). Fine for a directional map; treat t-stats near the noise floor as not-real.
  • EQ scores are relative-ranking only. The reference values are design-time sketches, not human-validated EQ-Bench annotations. Rank configs with them; don't quote them as absolute EQ.
  • Math partial-credit inflates close-but-wrong answers and is kept strictly secondary, never in the aggregate — the same grader-gaming the Gemma page documents at length.
  • MC is ceilinged — its signal is one-directional (breakage only). A harder knowledge battery would be needed for a positive MC result.
  • 4-bit weights. The exact crater/booster boundaries may shift at 8-bit (≈ an octave more noise headroom); the zones should hold.
